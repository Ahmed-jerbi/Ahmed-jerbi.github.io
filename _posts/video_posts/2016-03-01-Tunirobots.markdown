# Tunirobots | Tunisian National Day of Robotics

TuniRobots is a Univesity club association founded in 2010 and hosting since the competition of the National Day of Dobotics at INSAT (TN) [TUNIROBOTS](https://www.facebook.com/TUNIROBOTS21/).

I’ve been lucky to be part of the  orga team for several years — helping make the magic happen behind the scenes.

- Backstage Ops: live show AV management, live streaming and display tech.
- Front stage: performing music and lights shows.
- Media prod: Shot, edited, and reöeased different kinds of content: event highlights, promos, short films..etc.
- Loved the combo of tech, creativity, and chaos that comes with University events.
