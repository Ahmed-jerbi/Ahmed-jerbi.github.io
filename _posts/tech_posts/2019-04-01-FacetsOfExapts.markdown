# Facets of Expatriation - Augmented Photography Exhibition
## Washington State University -  April, 2019
Mobile application development - AR Photography Experience for artist [Mohsen Bchir](https://onoff.tasawar.net/mohsen-bchir-2).

________________________________________

Each photograph is recognized directly by the viewer’s camera using image-landmark detection, eliminating the need for visible markers or QR codes. This preserves the visual integrity and artistic purity of the exhibition.

An audio clip is then triggered augmenting the viewing experience to disover a story behind each subject.

The exhibition is titled "IT'S OKAY NOT TO UNDERSTAND" by artist [Mohsen Bchir](https://onoff.tasawar.net/mohsen-bchir-2).

### Tools: 
+  Unity: Vuforia SDK on android OS.
+  Adobe Suite for aftermovie production.
