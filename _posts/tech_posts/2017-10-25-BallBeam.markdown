# Ball & Beam |  PID Mechanical Mockup
University project for Proportional–integral–derivative controllers class.

_________________________________

Give the ball a push and the system will automatically regulate the beam to stabilize its position.

The real-time position is rendered in a Labview graph.


### Tools:

+ labVIEW (National Instruments)
+ Arduino
+ Ultrasonic Transducer
+ Servo Motor
