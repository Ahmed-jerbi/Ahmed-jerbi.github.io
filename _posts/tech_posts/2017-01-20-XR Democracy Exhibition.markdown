# XR Democracy Exhibition
Educational Exhibition for Democracy International - [www.democracyinternational.com](www.democracyinternational.com)

⚲ Hosted in Sousse, ElBirou Gallery. 

________________________________________

The exhibition uses art and technology to document historic events in the political and social movements of the Tunisian people for Democracy.


### Tools: 
+  VR: Unity, HTC Vive SDK
+  AR: Vuforia 
+  Projection Mapping: Blender, Resolume Arena
+  Videography: Adobe Premiere
