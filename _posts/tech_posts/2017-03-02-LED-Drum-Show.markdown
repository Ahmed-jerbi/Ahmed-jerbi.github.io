# LED DRUM SHOW | National Day of Robotics 2017
Created and performed for the National Day of Dobotics INSAT (TN) [TUNIROBOTS](https://www.facebook.com/TUNIROBOTS21/).
A mix of LED structures (strips, infinity mirror, panels) interacting with electronic drums and live audio.

### Tools:
+ Arduino Mega
+ RGB Leds & Power Circuit.
+ FFT Washers
+ Electronic Drums
