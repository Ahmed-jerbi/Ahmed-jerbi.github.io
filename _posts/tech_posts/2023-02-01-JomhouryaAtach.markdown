# Thirst Republic - جمهورية العطش
Developed the Augmented Photography Experience for artist [Mohsen Bchir](https://onoff.tasawar.net/mohsen-bchir-2)

________________________________________

The application hosts an audio bank documenting sounds, voices and testimonies about fresh water shortage in the region of Nefza.

Specific audio tracks are triggered for each photography frame detected by the viewer's camera.

The project is open-source and hosted on: [https://github.com/Ahmed-jerbi/JomhouryaAtach](https://github.com/Ahmed-jerbi/JomhouryaAtach)

### Tools: 
+  Unity: Vuforia SDK
+  Adobe Audition
